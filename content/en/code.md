Code and data from my research are shared on [GitHub](https://github.com/e-choque) so that results can be reproduced.

## Repositories

- **[NYC-Crime](https://github.com/e-choque/NYC-Crime)**. Data and code for fully reproducible analyses of crime in New York City.
- **[CensusBO2024](https://github.com/e-choque/CensusBO2024)**. How to process REDATAM data from Bolivia's 2024 Census in R. *R*.
- **[MPI_Bolivia](https://github.com/e-choque/MPI_Bolivia)**. Do-files to build a Multidimensional Poverty Index for Bolivia. *Stata*.
- **[Poverty_Bolivia](https://github.com/e-choque/Poverty_Bolivia)**. Do-files for the article "Determinantes microeconómicos de la pobreza en Bolivia". *Stata*.
- **[Fertility_and_laborOutcomes](https://github.com/e-choque/Fertility_and_laborOutcomes)**. Do-files for my undergraduate thesis on fertility and labor outcomes. *Stata*.
- **[fertility_and_wealth](https://github.com/e-choque/fertility_and_wealth)**. Fertility and wealth: causal evidence for Latin America. *R*.
- **[Effects-of-exports-on-regional-GDP](https://github.com/e-choque/Effects-of-exports-on-regional-GDP)**. Exports and regional GDP in Bolivia. *R*.
- **[UAH_MacroII](https://github.com/e-choque/UAH_MacroII)**. Dynare and MATLAB files for the computational project in Macroeconomics II (Master in Economics, UAH). *MATLAB*.
